Key caveats
Schedule sensitivity: The headline weekly impacts figure uses a 10s spot / 0s break schedule. A 10s:50s schedule reduces total weekly impacts by ~35%. Always confirm the schedule when comparing figures across sources.
Data week: All figures are from one week in March 2026. Route releases new data quarterly. Reach is stable year-round; impacts may vary seasonally.
Digital vs classic: Digital frames are only 5% of inventory (20,214 of 374,552) but reach the majority of adults due to concentration in high-traffic locations.
